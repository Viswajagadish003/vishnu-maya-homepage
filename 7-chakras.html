Learn About the 7 Chakras

A gentle introduction to the seven energy centres, what each one is linked with, and everyday ways to bring them back into harmony.

Oct 05, 2026 Vishnumaya Direct 10 min read Spiritual Practices

Introduction to the 7 Chakras

In Indian yogic thought, the body is described as more than flesh and bone. Running alongside it is a subtle system of energy, and the seven chakras are its main meeting points. Each one is linked with a part of the body, a set of feelings and a stage of inner growth.

Getting to know these seven centres gives you a simple map for noticing how you feel. With that map, small daily habits such as quiet sitting, slow breathing and mindful routines become easier to choose and easier to keep.

❧ॐ☙

What Are Chakras?

The word chakra comes from Sanskrit and means "wheel". Old yoga and Ayurveda texts picture these wheels spinning at points along the spine, from its base up to the top of the head. When energy moves freely through them, a person is said to feel steady in body, calm in mind and open in spirit. You do not need to hold any particular belief to find them useful: many people simply treat the chakras as a set of prompts for checking in with themselves.

❧ॐ☙

The Seven Chakras at a Glance

1. Root Chakra

Safety, stability and feeling grounded

2. Sacral Chakra

Creativity, feelings and close bonds

3. Solar Plexus Chakra

Willpower, confidence and self-worth

4. Heart Chakra

Love, kindness and forgiveness

5. Throat Chakra

Honest speech and being heard

6. Third Eye Chakra

Insight, intuition and inner sight

7. Crown Chakra

Connection, purpose and higher awareness
